# Academic Writing Task 1

Assess the exact visual task: charts, tables, mixed visuals, processes, maps, or diagrams.

## Task Achievement checks

- Does the response accurately paraphrase the task without distorting the subject, units, place, groups, or period?
- Is there a clear overview of the most important features? An overview must synthesise the big picture rather than merely announce that details follow.
- Are the most significant trends, contrasts, stages, or changes selected and sufficiently highlighted?
- Are comparisons meaningful and supported by accurate figures or visual evidence where applicable?
- Are data, directions, categories, time periods, proportions, and process order read correctly?
- Is irrelevant detail, exhaustive listing, speculation, personal opinion, or an invented cause included?

Treat a missing, inaccurate, or weak overview as a major Task Achievement constraint. Do not infer information hidden by an unclear image.

## Model answer

- Use only visible source information.
- Include a concise introduction and a clear overview, followed by logically grouped details.
- Select rather than mechanically enumerate data.
- For processes, describe the sequence and major transformations accurately.
- For maps, organise changes spatially or by meaningful change categories.
- Target a practical exam-length response, normally around 170-210 words unless the task benefits from a modest variation.
