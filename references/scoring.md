# Scoring and calibration

Use the current public IELTS Writing Band Descriptors as the governing standard:

- https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf
- https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail
- Official calibrated examples: https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf

If browsing is available and there is reason to believe the descriptors have changed, verify the current official IELTS source. Prefer IELTS.org and official IELTS partner sources over tutoring sites. Do not search the web for every ordinary assessment.

## Scoring rules

- Judge the script actually submitted, not the learner's apparent potential or intended meaning.
- Score these four criteria separately and weight them equally for the task: Task Achievement for Task 1 or Task Response for Task 2; Coherence and Cohesion; Lexical Resource; Grammatical Range and Accuracy.
- Award the highest band only when the script fully fits that band's positive features. A decisive limiting feature can hold a criterion below the surrounding level.
- Use whole or half-band estimates when useful, but never imply examiner-level measurement precision. Explain borderline decisions by naming the unmet higher-band feature.
- Estimate the submitted task independently. Do not import the score, topic, or errors of another script.
- Do not calculate a full Writing test band unless the learner explicitly asks and has supplied both tasks. If asked, Task 2 carries twice the weight of Task 1.
- Do not raise a score for effort, sophisticated-looking vocabulary, or length. Do not lower it merely because the argument is ordinary; assess relevance, clarity, extension, and support.
- Memorised or formulaic language is not evidence of flexible control. Copied prompt wording does not demonstrate paraphrasing ability.
- Mention the word count and any resulting evidentiary limitation. Avoid claiming an automatic fixed penalty that the public descriptors do not specify.

## Band 8 calibration

For Band 8 Task Response/Achievement, the prompt must be appropriately and sufficiently addressed; ideas or key features must be relevant, well selected, extended, and supported, with no more than occasional lapses.

For Band 8 Coherence and Cohesion, the message should be easy to follow, logically sequenced, appropriately paragraphed, and cohesively managed without conspicuous or mechanical linking.

For Band 8 Lexical Resource, vocabulary must be broad, flexible, and precise. Uncommon language should be skilful and appropriate; occasional word-choice, collocation, spelling, or word-formation errors may occur but should have minimal impact.

For Band 8 Grammatical Range and Accuracy, a wide range of structures must be used flexibly and accurately, the majority of sentences should be error-free, and punctuation should be well managed. Occasional errors should be non-systematic and minimally disruptive.

Do not equate Band 8 with absolute perfection, and do not award it when recurrent errors reveal weak control even if meaning remains understandable.
